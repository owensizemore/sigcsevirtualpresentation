## Why This Course

* AI is having a crazy impact on software development (and all disciplines!)
* We both feel **strongly** that we can't just keep teaching what we've been teaching by ignoring AI
* We have more questions than answers
  - How should software engineering adapt?
  - What are the essential human skills that cannot be replaced? 
* We are skeptical about teaching "best practices"
  - Rather we aim to offer you a safe playground for practicing,
    and learn what's best for you

## Course Structure

* Two projects: **starter** (solo work, paired help) $\to$ **team** (big)
* Weekly cycle:
  * **reflect** $\to$ discuss $\to$ plan/**predict** $\to$ <br> build/verify/debug $\to$ **reflect** $\to \cdots$
* No traditional homework or exams
  - Final exam slot will be for project demos

---

## The Starter Project

* **Individual** project, as a warm-up
  - Try something new that you are really interested in
  - Keep it small first, but could evolve it later
* Still, you'll work with 1-2 human partners
  - Cross-pollinators, sounding boards, and collaborators who learn from each other
  - You may even choose to work on the same idea
  - But you are each responsible for your own system
* Share what you build in Week 6
* 10% of course grade

---

## The Team Project

* Yes, the BIG one
  - We want it to be more ambitious than anything you tried before in **any** class
  - Don't be afraid to experiment and fail
  - We grade efforts in planning and analysis, not on the particulars of implementation or specific features
* Work in a **team of 5-6**
  - You are jointly responsible for the project
* Share what you build at the final exam slot (Sat. Dec. 12 2-5pm)
* 60% of course grade, broken down into deliverables over time with $\le$ 10% each 

---

## A Typical Week - Part I

* From last Thursday: continue working on your project
  - But with AI agents doing most of the grunt work 🤞
* Tuesday 9am: **Reflection** due
  - What I did / what I understood vs. delegated / what I predicted vs. reality / what suprised me / what I'd do differently
  - No AI; a scanned handwritten page expected
  - 10% of course grade; can miss 2
* Tuesday meeting
  - Share/reflect as a group
  - Instructors introduce useful concepts/techniques/perspectives
  - Aim to understand and improve

---

## A Typical Week - Part II

* Thursday meeting
  - Plan and build together
  - Aim to make effective use of AI 
* End of Thursday meeting: **Prediction** due
  - What I'm about to try / what should work / what might break / how long
  - No AI when writing this
  - 10% of course grade; can miss 2
* To next Tuesday: continue working on your project
  - Again, with AI doing the grunt work

## Icebreaker!

* 5 minutes: briefly answer the following questions

  1. How did you become interested in Computer Science?

  <!--- What, if any, steps do you take to deliberately plan and design the code you write?-->
  3. How do you see yourself using your Computer Science skills in the future?
  4. What is one small thing in your daily life that software could just handle for you, but doesn't exist (or existing products are not to your preferences)?
  
* 5 minutes: Share your answers with a neighbor (pairs or 3)
